# Writing style

- Do NOT use long dashes (em dash `—` or en dash `–`). Use a comma, parentheses, or a plain hyphen `-` instead.
- Avoid AI buzzwords and filler words such as: seamless, leverage, robust, elevate, delve, unlock, empower, game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world". Write plainly and directly.
- These rules apply to all generated prose and documents (HTML, contracts, emails, reports, etc.).
